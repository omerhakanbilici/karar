Karar

Karar - a Mac app for Ollaya

Ask typed questions about any text and see calibrated answers while you type. Karar bundles the Ollaya engine for decision models, downloads models for you and re-runs the questions every time you pause typing. Everything runs on your Mac.

Download for Mac

Apple silicon · macOS 14+ · All releases

What it does

Decision models don't write text. They read a text and answer typed questions about it: a choice (refund, technical help, …), a score (2.4 of 3) or a yes/no, each with a calibrated confidence.

  • Live answers while you type: each edit re-runs the questions a moment after you pause.
  • Editable question cards (Advanced): change instructions and options, add choice, score and yes/no questions.
  • An inspector: which model answered and why, timings, token usage, the raw response, Copy JSON and Copy as curl.
  • A token counter under the text, and a warning when the text is longer than the model can read.
  • Pins: ⌘↩ keeps a text and its answers in the sidebar, to compare them later.
  • A native SwiftUI app that follows your Mac's light or dark appearance.

First launch

Karar is not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm once that you want to open it.

  1. Open Karar.dmg and drag Karar to Applications.

  2. Open Karar. macOS says it could not verify the app. Click Done (on macOS 14: OK).

  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to “Karar.app” was blocked and click Open Anyway, then confirm. Karar opens, and from now on it opens like any other app.

Karar then asks which model to download. laya is recommended: fast, 100+ languages, about 1.5 GB.
